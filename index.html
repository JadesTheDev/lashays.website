About Me

Photography has been my passion for over 14 years. It all began with my daughter, when I discovered how much I loved capturing the little moments and expressions that made her special.

Over time, that passion grew into photographing families, special occasions, and the beauty of the world around me. I love helping people see how beautiful they truly are.

I've learned that the perfect photo takes patience, creativity, and a willingness to try different angles and lighting. I never gave up, and I continue to grow with every photograph I take.
